# PNGToolbox Product Truth

PNGToolbox is a browser-local image utility for people who need to convert WebP files to PNG or remove solid backgrounds from JPG, PNG, and WebP images without an account.

The primary success action is simple: choose a task, add an image, adjust only the settings needed, inspect the measured result, and download it. Existing local processing, batch conversion, transparency measurement, SEO content, privacy wording, legal pages, and accessible native controls are product requirements.
