The fill colour

Why the matte choice matters more than it sounds

Transparency in a PNG is per-pixel, and many pixels are partly transparent — the anti-aliased edge around a cutout, a soft shadow, a faded logo edge. JPG has no fourth channel to store that, so each partly transparent pixel is blended with the fill colour and the result is baked in. Choose white and a cutout destined for a black website grows a light fringe around every edge; choose black and the same cutout on white paper looks dirty. That is the entire reason the fill colour is a choice here instead of a silent default.

WhiteMarketplace product images, most print workflows, email. Amazon, Etsy and similar platforms expect a pure white background — and reject listings without one.
BlackAssets destined for dark-mode interfaces, overlays, and video thumbnails where the surrounding UI is dark.
CustomMatching a brand background exactly. Pick the colour from your design tool — a near-match produces a visible rectangle against the real background.

If the destination needs the cutout to stay a cutout, no fill colour helps: JPG is the wrong target, and PNG or WebP is required. Our alpha and transparency guide explains what each format does with semi-transparent pixels, and the white background tool flattens PNGs while keeping the PNG format.

Questions

PNG to JPG FAQ

Why did my logo get bigger after converting to JPG?

PNG stores flat-colour graphics with extreme efficiency; JPEG stores every 8×8 block of pixels with frequency data regardless of content. On graphics and logos the measured result is routinely 4 to 10 times larger — and it loses transparency on top. Keep such images as PNG.

What fill colour should I choose for marketplace photos?

White. Marketplaces standardise on pure white backgrounds, and a JPG exported with the white matte matches that expectation exactly. Check the specific platform's help pages for their current rules before listing.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Conversion runs locally in this browser with the canvas API — your files are never transmitted to a server.

Can I resize while converting?

Yes. Set a width or height and the whole batch follows, with each file keeping its own aspect ratio when the lock is enabled.

Does converting to JPG remove metadata?

Yes, as a side effect: the output is re-encoded from pixels, so EXIF metadata in the source PNG does not survive. The report shows exactly what was produced.
