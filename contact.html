Reporting a conversion problem

Most WebP conversion issues can be diagnosed without sending the original file. A report that includes the following usually gets an answer in one reply rather than several:

  • The page URL and the tool you used (converter or background remover).
  • Your browser and its version, and your operating system.
  • The source format — a standard WebP, an animated WebP, or something renamed to .webp.
  • The approximate source file size and pixel dimensions.
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead — including the exact wording of any message shown.
  • Whether the problem also occurs after a hard reload with the cache bypassed.

If a browser error is involved, the browser's developer console may contain a useful line. Copying that text is more helpful than a screenshot and costs you nothing in privacy.

About sending image files

You do not need to attach your image. Conversion runs locally in your browser, so there is no server-side copy we could compare against. If a specific file is genuinely necessary to reproduce a defect, send a throwaway test image rather than the original you care about — never a document, an identity photo, or anything confidential.

Corrections and guide feedback

If a guide is unclear, uses outdated wording, or links to something that has moved, send the page URL and the correction you would suggest. Practical, specific corrections — a wrong pixel size, a step that no longer matches the interface — are especially welcome, and are usually incorporated with the next revision of that page.

Advertising and business enquiries

Privacy and data requests

Because images are processed in your browser and never uploaded, there is no stored image data to access or delete. For anything relating to analytics or correspondence you have sent, write to the address above and describe what you need. The privacy policy explains what is and is not collected in more detail.

Response times

Replies are sent as time allows, usually within a few days. Bug reports with the details listed above are handled first. Before submitting a converted file anywhere important, keep your original and check the exported dimensions yourself.
