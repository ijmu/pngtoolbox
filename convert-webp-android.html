Questions

Android FAQ

Where do converted files go on Android?

Chrome saves downloads to your phone's Downloads folder — visible in the Files app or any gallery that scans Downloads. The ZIP option keeps a whole batch in one file, which you can then move or share anywhere.

Why convert WebP at all if Android displays it?

Android and Chrome display WebP natively, but plenty of other systems don't: upload forms, marketplaces, print shops, email attachments, and older office software often reject it. PNG and JPG are accepted almost everywhere.

Why use a website instead of an app?

No installation, no permissions, no account — and your images are processed on your own device rather than uploaded to a conversion server.

Can I convert multiple WebP files at once on Android?

Yes. Select several files at once, convert the batch, and download everything as a single ZIP archive.
