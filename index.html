FAQ

Questions, answered

Are my WebP files uploaded to a server?

No. Decoding, conversion, and ZIP packaging all happen locally in your browser. Your files are never transmitted anywhere.

Does the PNG keep transparency?

Yes. When the WebP source contains transparent pixels, the converted PNG preserves the alpha channel. The report shows the real output so you can verify it.

Why is the PNG larger than my WebP?

PNG is lossless and keeps every pixel exact, while WebP usually applies stronger compression. That's normal — PNG trades file size for universal compatibility and quality.

Can I convert many files at once?

Yes. Add as many WebP files as you like, convert the whole batch, and download everything as one ZIP archive.

Can I convert WebP to JPG instead of PNG?

Yes. Switch the output format to JPG and pick a quality level. JPG files are smaller and work everywhere, but they can't store transparency — transparent areas are filled with white.

Can I resize during conversion?

Yes. Set an optional output width and height before converting. Keep the ratio lock enabled to avoid stretching.

What about animated WebP files?

This converter is designed for still images. Animated WebP files may only expose a single frame depending on your browser's decoder.

Is there a limit on file count or size?

No accounts, credits, or artificial caps. Very large files depend on your device's memory, but the tool itself imposes no limits.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The layout adapts to phones and tablets, and file selection works with the normal system picker.
