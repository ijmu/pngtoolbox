FAQ

Before you convert

Are my WebP files uploaded to a server?

No. Decoding, conversion, measurement, and ZIP packaging run locally in your browser. Your image files are not sent to a PNGToolbox server.

Does PNG output keep transparency?

Yes. If the WebP source has transparent pixels, PNG output preserves them. JPG output replaces transparent pixels with the background color you choose.

Why can PNG output be larger than WebP?

PNG uses lossless compression, while WebP can use stronger lossy or lossless compression. The converter shows the actual size of every result.

What are the WebP and file-size limits?

The converter is designed for still WebP images. Animated files may expose only one frame, each input is limited to 20 MB, and very large batches depend on available device memory.
